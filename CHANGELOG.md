# Changelog

All notable changes to Moving & Storage Volume Calculator are listed here.
The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the
project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-08

### Added

- Room-by-room moving inventory: 441 items in 21 rooms and 5 categories, with search and custom items.
- 14 Quick Starts, from "Boxes only" to "5 bed", garage, storage unit, office, shop and café.
- Live total in m³ or ft³, with items, boxes, estimated weight and a packing margin.
- Best-fit van or truck, shipping container and storage unit, for the UK, Europe, the United States and Australia.
- Box estimator: five quick questions suggest a count for each box size.
- Quote form with a forgotten-rooms reminder, move details, consent and a confirmation screen.
- Share link, branded print copy, and the list remembered in the visitor's browser.
- Branded quote request email, with an optional copy for the customer.
- Leads: statuses, follow-up reminders, notes, timeline, quick contact actions, CSV and PDF export.
- Failed-email flag with "Send email again", duplicate merging and rate limiting.
- Staff access to Leads for Editors, privacy exporter and eraser, and automatic clean-up.
- Consent record on every lead: when the customer agreed, the exact wording and the privacy policy link shown, also in the CSV and the personal data export.
- Volume Calculator block and `[msvcalc_calculator]` shortcode, light, dark or automatic theme.
